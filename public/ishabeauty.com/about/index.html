Isha Beauty

At Isha Beauty, it's all about "Feeling Beautiful"

At Isha Beauty, we are dedicated to offering high-performing, results-driven treatments that are meticulously tailored to rejuvenate and protect your skin. Our services are carefully crafted to prevent and reduce the signs of aging, helping you achieve a radiant, healthy complexion. We emphasize clean, glowing skin, ensuring each treatment is personalized to meet your unique skincare needs and goals, leaving you with long-lasting confidence in your natural beauty.

Under the expert guidance of Shaily Toor, a certified beautician with a wide array of specialized skills, Isha Beauty combines the best of both modern aesthetic technology and traditional hands-on methods. Shaily’s extensive experience in microblading, Asian bridal makeup, microneedling, dermaplaning, Russian eyelash extensions, and more ensures that every treatment is designed to deliver visible, transformative results. Her proficiency in advanced techniques like LED therapy, chemical peels, biopeeling, and hydra and oxygen peels empowers us to use cutting-edge technology to stimulate your skin’s natural biological mechanisms, backed by clinical evidence, for the most effective skincare solutions.

Whether you’re looking to enhance your brows with microblading, undergo a luxurious facial with microneedling or dermaplaning, or experience a rejuvenating Indian head massage, we offer a comprehensive range of treatments designed to meet your individual needs. We also offer specialized services like spray tanning, acne treatment, and eyelash perming to help you look and feel your best, every day.

 

Shaily Toor

Founder, Licensed Aesthetician, And Skin Transformation Coach

Shaily Toor, the visionary behind Isha Salon, is a certified beautician with an extensive range of expertise in beauty treatments. With numerous certifications and years of hands-on experience, Shaily excels in a variety of skills, including microblading, Asian bridal makeup, ear piercing, hopi ear candling, microneedling, hairstyling, spray tanning, Russian eyelash extensions, dermaplaning, LED therapy, acne treatment and renaissance peels, chemical peels, biopeeling, hydra and oxygen peels, Indian head massage, gel polish nails, and eyelash perming.

Her passion for beauty and wellness is reflected in the personalized treatments she offers at Isha Salon, where each client experiences her unwavering dedication to achieving beautiful, radiant results. Shaily’s approach combines her in-depth knowledge of modern beauty techniques with a genuine desire to enhance her clients’ natural features. With her expertise, she has established Isha Salon as a trusted destination for transformative beauty experiences, leaving clients not only feeling rejuvenated but also empowered.
