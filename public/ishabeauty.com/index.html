Our Story

At Isha Salon, owner Shaily Toor has crafted an elegant retreat where personalized beauty and wellness are at the forefront. With locations in both Shoreditch and Bethnal, London, Isha Salon offers an oasis for those seeking tailored beauty treatments in a relaxing environment. Shaily believes that true beauty is nurtured through individualized care and consistent attention to detail, both at the salon and at home.

From luxurious hair treatments and revitalizing facials to expert eyebrow and eyelash services, precise hair removal, and soothing massages, Isha Salon’s offerings are designed to enhance your natural beauty and sense of well-being. Whether you’re visiting to refresh your look, indulge in a bit of self-care, or simply relax, Isha Salon is here to provide a memorable experience that’s uniquely yours. Discover the artistry and care behind every treatment at Isha Salon, where every client’s beauty journey is thoughtfully guided.

Why Chose Us?

We know that London offers many options for beauty care, but at Isha Salon, we go beyond traditional service. Here’s what makes us unique:

  • Customized Care: Each treatment plan is personalized to fit your style, goals, and preferences, so you leave feeling like your best self.
  • Professional Expertise: Our team is skilled, experienced, and up-to-date on the latest in beauty trends and techniques, ensuring you’re in trusted hands.
  • Luxury and Comfort: Our salon spaces in Shoreditch and Bethnal are designed to offer you a calming retreat, where you can relax in style.
  • Commitment to Wellness: We believe that beauty is enhanced by a holistic approach. That’s why we focus on treatments that nurture your skin, hair, and spirit, from the inside out.
