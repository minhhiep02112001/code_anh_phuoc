Response time

We aim to reply within a few working days. Update suggestions are processed continuously, so the listing may refresh before you hear back.

What we won't reply to

Cold sales pitches, generic guest-post offers, and SEO link-exchange requests get archived politely without a reply. We're a small team — please be specific.
