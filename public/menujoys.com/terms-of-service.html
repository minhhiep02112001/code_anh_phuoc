Section 01

Acceptance of Terms

By using Menujoys you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the site. These terms apply to everyone — diners browsing menus, visitors suggesting updates, and restaurant owners managing listings.

Section 02

The Service

Menujoys is a global, English-first menu discovery platform. We help diners explore local restaurants by surfacing menus, prices when available, opening hours, and useful local food details. We are not a delivery marketplace, a booking engine, or a review platform.

The service includes the website at menujoys.com and any subdomain we publish (for example, restaurant-name.menujoys.com), as well as content, brand, and editorial output.

Eligibility & Accounts

You can browse Menujoys at any age in most regions. To submit forms, claim a listing, or otherwise act on behalf of a business, you must be at least 18 years old and authorized to represent the business in question.

If you create or manage an account, you're responsible for keeping the credentials safe. Tell us within 5 working days if you believe your account has been accessed without authorisation.

You retain ownership of original content (your photos, your menus, your descriptions). You can request removal at any time via the contact form.

Restaurant Listings

Menujoys may publish a listing for a restaurant based on public information, owner submissions, or visitor suggestions. Restaurant owners can claim their listing to manage menu details, hours, and contact info.

  • Menujoys reserves the right to remove listings that violate the Content Policy.
  • Closures, rebrands, and ownership transfers are handled per the dispute-resolution flow described in the Content Policy.

Paid Tiers (Studio & Atelier)

Adding and claiming a basic listing is free. Optional paid tiers (Studio, Atelier) are billed monthly or annually per location. Pricing and feature inclusions are described on the For Restaurants page.

  • You may cancel a paid tier at any time — your listing stays on the free plan.
  • Refunds: pro-rated for annual plans within the first 14 days; otherwise non-refundable.
  • We may change pricing for future billing cycles with 30 days' notice.
  • Taxes are calculated at checkout based on your billing region.

Section 09

DMCA & Takedowns

Menujoys responds to valid copyright takedown requests under the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent regimes elsewhere.

Submitting a takedown

Send a notice through the contact form with the topic "Copyright / takedown" and include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • Identification of the material on Menujoys that allegedly infringes — full URL preferred.
  • Your contact details (name, address, email).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are authorised to act.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notice

If material you posted has been taken down and you believe in good faith that the takedown was a mistake, you may file a counter-notice via the same channel.
