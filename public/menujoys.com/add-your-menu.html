Common Mistakes

A few do's and don'ts.

Submissions that follow these usually clear review on the first pass.

Do — group items into clear sections

Coffee, Cold Drinks, Brunch, Pastries… one heading per group. Helps editors and diners scan.

Do — share prices when stable

Even one price per section helps. Mark seasonal or "market price" items clearly.

Do — flag your popular dishes

Tell us which 3–5 items diners actually order. We'll feature them as popular picks.

Don't — paste 2,000 words of marketing copy

Editorial format is short and useful. Long love-letters get trimmed before publishing.

Don't — submit a low-resolution photo of paper

Blurry or rotated paper menus take longer to transcribe. Type plainly into the form when you can.

Don't — submit two pages for the same address

One unique address = one Menujoys page. Multiple branches each get their own listing.
