Why Menujoys

Onboarding

From submission to a live page.

Most restaurants are live within 2 working days. Here's the actual flow.

01

Add or claim your listing

Submit a fresh menu, or claim the listing your restaurant already has. Both flows take ~5 minutes.

02

Editor review

A real editor checks the menu, prices, and details. They may email if something looks off — never invent.

03

Editorial polish

We format your menu in the Menujoys editorial style — categories, popular picks, dietary labels.

04

Publish & share

Your page goes live on a clean subdomain. We email the link with a short share kit you can drop in newsletters.

05

Quarterly refresh

We send a quick reminder every 3 months to confirm prices and items are still current.

06

Always-on updates

Menu changes, new dishes, holiday hours — all updatable in minutes through your claimed dashboard.
